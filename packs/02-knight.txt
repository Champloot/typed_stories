# The knight: a short story. Format: see FORMAT.md
#
# The king sends you after the dragon of the Grey Hills. Along the road the
# details decide: a beggar's eyes, a troll's crown, a wolf's paw. What you did
# on the road comes back at the lair and at the castle afterwards, and the
# story ends one of several ways (see the 'finale' lines).
# Marks: +1 good, 0 does not matter, -1 bad, -2 really bad.

pack: The Knight
style: ornate
verdicts: Dragon-food. | Peasant with a sword. | Worthy knight. | The bards will sing of you.
phases: Castle | Road | Tavern | Forest | Lair | Homecoming
route: fixed
meters: Honor 5 | Gold 5 | Health 6
ending: Honor | The heralds stop saying your name. Then the stable boy does. You hand in your spurs and become a turnip farmer. The turnips do not judge.
ending: Gold | You sell your horse, then your sword, then your boots. A knight with no boots is just a man in a tin hat. You walk home.
ending: Health | You lie down under an oak "just for a moment". A passing monk takes you to the abbey. You will be fine. The quest will not.

# How the story ends when you live through it. The 'finale_if' lines are
# checked first, in order; otherwise the rating picks one, worst first.
finale: Keeper of the Potatoes | You come back with no dragon, no gold and half a cape. The king gives you a new post: guarding the royal potatoes. You guard them well. Nobody steals a single one.
finale: A Modest Return | You come back alive, which surprises the court. A statue is commissioned in your honor. It is very small, and the pigeons love it.
finale: Hero of the Realm | Bells ring as you ride in. The princess smiles, the king pays in full, and the jester writes a joke about you that is actually funny.
finale: A Legend | They sing of you in every tavern from here to the sea. Most of the songs are even true. Children play at being you, and the dragon plays along.
finale_if: dragon_friend | The Dragon Bakery | You ride home on the dragon. The king faints. A month later 'Ember & Sir' opens in the square: the dragon bakes, you sell. The bread is always warm, and nobody ever robs the place.
finale_if: mocked_troll, bard_follows | The Ballad of the Small Troll | The bard saw everything. Now every child in the kingdom sings about the knight who beat a troll in a paper crown. You grow a beard and move to the mountains. The song gets there first.
finale_if: dragon_reads, fed_hermit | The Library Under the Hill | The dragon opens the realm's first library, and the old beggar with the starry beard becomes its keeper. Late books are fined one sheep a week. You get a card for life, and a quiet seat by the warm wall.

# A level's medals: won at the end of a day lived through, when all parts hold.
medal: Paragon | meters >= 8
medal: By a Hair | meters <= 2
medal: The Dragon Bakery | finale The Dragon Bakery
medal: A Legend | finale A Legend
