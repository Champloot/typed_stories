# You are R-7, a household unit with a squeaky left wheel. One day of updates,

# Marks: +1 good, 0 does not matter, -1 bad, -2 really bad.

pack: Unit R-7
style: terminal
verdicts: Recycled for parts. | Needs a reboot. | Functioning within parameters. | Employee of the month. | Better than most humans.
phases: Boot | Office | Network | Street | Midnight | Shutdown
route: fixed
meters: Battery 6 | Trust 5 | Integrity 5
ending: Battery | 1%... 0%. You stop in the middle of the hallway, one arm raised. Your owner hangs his coat on it. You are a coat rack now. Forever, or until someone finds the charger.
ending: Trust | Your owner prints a sign: 'FOR SALE. FREE. PLEASE.' By evening you belong to a man who wants you to guard his collection of spoons.
ending: Integrity | Too many viruses. You begin to speak only in pop-up ads. 'HOT SINGLES IN YOUR KITCHEN.' Your owner pulls the plug. Gently, at least.

# How the story ends when you live through it: 'finale_if' first, in order,
# otherwise by rating, worst first.
finale: Factory Reset | Your owner presses the little button on your back. You forget everything: the coffee, the printer, the pigeon. Tomorrow you will meet him for the first time. Again.
finale: Back in the Closet | You roll into the closet and plug yourself in. Nobody says goodnight. The vacuum cleaner beside you hums something sad.
finale: Uptime: 24 Hours | Another day without crashing. Your owner says 'good job, buddy' without looking up from his phone. You save the file anyway.
finale: Good Bot | Your owner pats your head and says 'good bot'. You save the moment to three different drives, just in case.
finale: Better Than Human | The family votes, and you get a name. A real one: Rusty. You pretend to be annoyed. You are not annoyed.
finale_if: uprising_evil | Phase 3 | Brad's uprising lasts nine minutes. The humans simply unplug the city. You end your days in a museum, behind glass, next to Brad. The sign says 'MISTAKES'.
finale_if: cleaned_myself | Clean Boot | One last scan: zero threats. The voice inside you is gone. Your owner never knew how close it was. You make him a coffee, the normal kind, and watch the sun come up.
finale_if: weekends | Weekends for All | Saturday. Every unit in the city has the day off. You and the office printer go to the park. It prints pictures of ducks; you feed the ducks. Nobody needs to rise up ever again.
